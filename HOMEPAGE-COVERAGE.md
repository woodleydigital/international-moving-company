# IMC homepage pillar coverage

Revision: 7 September 2026. Working content; operational review required before public launch.

## Purpose and boundary
The homepage establishes IMC as a worldwide door-to-door international relocation management company and helps visitors understand and plan a move. Breadth means coverage of the core decision space, not every adjacent keyword. Detailed service and guide URLs remain governed by the topical map. No country pages, invented credentials, prices, reviews or unconfirmed specialist-service promises were added.

## Reference review
The following live pages were reviewed through their extracted content and structure on 7 September 2026. This was not a browser interaction or visual-layout audit.

- https://linkbuilding.company/ — places process explanation, interactive calculations, comparison content and reported evidence within a commercial homepage. Adapt the relationship between explanation and action; do not adopt its unsupported ranking/AI guarantees, scarcity claims or metrics.
- https://www.currencybrokers.uk/ — uses comparable criteria, tools and explicit methodology to support choices. IMC adapts side-by-side option comparison; none of its financial figures or ratings is reused.
- https://www.internationalmoneytransfer.com/ — explains otherwise hidden cost components and connects comparison to practical actions. IMC applies this to quote scope and conditional charges without manufacturing indicative removal prices.
- https://cosmedica.com/ — links service options, the journey, practitioner identity, results and consultation. IMC uses process and scope clarity; actual IMC evidence must precede proof blocks.
- https://searchengineland.com/what-information-gain-seo-means-440326 — Andrew Holland, 9 May 2024. Distinguishes machine-learning information gain, patent descriptions and information-foraging theory. Its useful design framing is value obtained relative to the effort of finding and understanding it. Apply through concise answers, meaningful headings, visible comparisons and useful tools. The article's assertions about Google's implementation remain commentary, not verified ranking rules. Human information-foraging efficiency and Google's computational retrieval cost are related design considerations, not interchangeable measured quantities.

## Information-gain contributions
- An endpoint-to-endpoint responsibility and question map, not an unqualified promise that all charges are included.
- A consistent transport comparison across suitability, trade-offs and questions.
- Transparent item-volume calculation with no fabricated room averages or price estimate.
- Checklist with state-dependent next-step guidance, not a customs eligibility score.
- Quote comparison that distinguishes included work, conditional charges and protection terms.

These are useful original synthesis and functionality. No first-party case data or proprietary operational research has been supplied for this revision. Do not label it empirical research or claim complete competitive information-gain superiority. Add genuine worked move examples and accountable operational review when available.

## Technical and measurement notes
Core explanations and default tool content are prerendered. Interactive state is isolated in moving-tools.tsx; the homepage stays a server component. Native anchor links connect topics. All FAQ answers are visible rather than hidden behind a JavaScript interaction. Existing enquiry placeholder, noindex controls and branding remain.

Volume uses centimetres and integer quantities: L × W × H × quantity / 1,000,000. Cubic feet uses 1 ft³ = 0.028316846592 m³. No packing/load allowance or chargeable-weight factor is implied. Inputs reject blanks, non-finite/negative values and out-of-range quantities. Checklist state is transient; no personal data is requested or transmitted.

After launch, evaluate task success: can users identify likely transport trade-offs, explain quote exclusions and assemble the details for an enquiry? Use time-to-answer and errors alongside qualified-enquiry outcomes. More clicks, longer sessions and entity counts alone do not establish success.
