## Changes to the working map

1. **Small shipments:** add a substantive small-move section to `/services/`, linked to shared-container guidance and the itemised quote flow. Explain furniture/carton inventory, quantity, estimated m³, timing flexibility and what happens after an enquiry. A separate small-move URL is conditional on a distinct, substantial brief; it is not required simply because the form has a size option.
2. **Pre-move assessment:** give inventory-led, video and in-person assessment clear ownership under `/how-it-works/`, retaining availability qualifications. The homepage already introduces this. A standalone survey page can be assessed later if the process page cannot serve the task well.
3. **Volume guidance:** move `/guides/estimating-moving-volume/` into the launch-priority supporting queue alongside the new inventory functionality. This changes planning priority, not publication status. Explain fixed item estimates versus measured dimensions, packing effects and final survey confirmation. Reuse the same 89-item dataset and arithmetic; do not create conflicting tools.
4. **Protection:** give the homepage/service/quote-comparison paths direct access to the existing moving-insurance guide proposal. Prioritise an operations-reviewed explanation for launch, without presenting IMC as an insurer or promising universal cover.
5. **Management role:** make coordinator responsibilities, partner handoffs and customer responsibilities explicit in services and process. Do not interpret the brand's relocation-management description as confirmation of immigration, tax, school-placement or home-search services.
6. **Trust:** combine principles and any substantiated credentials within About initially. Separate pages require enough real evidence and a distinct customer task.

Retain packing, storage, documents, restrictions, transit-time and delivery-day topics from the existing map. Their usefulness is not diminished by the reference sitemap omitting dedicated entries. Country, route and programmatic-page deferrals remain unchanged.

Avoid scattering near-identical shipping/removal/service pages or treating a larger URL count as authority. These are editorial and engineering choices, not a claimed measurable Google retrieval-cost formula.

## Targeted content checks and cautions

Four linked non-blog pages were also inspected through extracted content to assess ambiguous ownership. No live calculator interaction or backend verification was performed.

- [Full container](https://internationalmoving.services/sole-use-container): useful questions around loading, capacity, documents and delivery, but its broad no-delay language and universal-looking thresholds should not become IMC promises.
- [Groupage](https://internationalmoving.services/groupage): introduces shared capacity and flexibility. Its suggested volume boundary differs from the full-container page's framing; IMC should use shipment-specific assessment rather than transfer either threshold.
- [Consultant](https://internationalmoving.services/consultant): extends into immigration, housing, education and tax. Those are not confirmed IMC services.
- [Cost calculator](https://internationalmoving.services/cost-calculator): a separate pricing-tool destination exists. Its presence is not evidence that IMC has a reliable pricing model. Our supplied item-volume estimates are not freight prices.

Next planning deliverable: develop briefs for services, process, container options, costs and volume with operational ownership, proof needs and internal links. Keep utility/legal work as launch dependencies. This amendment does not rewrite the existing workbook or publish website content.
